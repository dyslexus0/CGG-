## Features

### Top-bar widgets

Choose what you want to see, including:

- Thread accuracy, counts to your accuracy targets, and accuracy after another mistake.
- Counts to the next split or GET, with pace-based time estimates.
- Rank-up distance, your lead, and your share of the thread's valid counts.
- Current and best streaks, plus counts to the next milestone.
- One-minute and five-minute CPM, session counts, mistakes, and accuracy.
- Today's counts, daily streaks, and personal records.
- The next English word in Letters Double, with distance and progress.

Drag widgets around the header, change their order, and adjust text sizes, colours, outlines, labels, and spacing. You can save different layouts for different threads.

### Stats

- An Overview of counts, accuracy, active days, and daily records.
- Longest and current streak leaderboards, saved streak history, and thread names.
- Streak distribution with completed/current totals and percentages for 100+ streaks.
- Speed progression showing how the fastest valid GET or split improved over time.
- Date and thread filters, graphs, and CSV exports where available.

Counter names use the site's colours and icons when available. Speed downloads save progress, support Pause/Resume, and retry temporary failures automatically.

## History setup

Full historical stats  need a one-time download of past counting days. You can check the download size during setup or open **History** on the Stats page later.

The initial download can be large. You can pause and resume it. Each raw file is removed after processing; compact summaries stay saved, so later updates can reuse completed days.

Daily statistics use **Eastern Time — America/New_York**, including daylight saving changes.

Live pace and session stats depend on the posts your browser observes. A streak marked **≥** has an unknown starting point and shows the minimum confirmed length. Streak distribution includes runs of at least 100 counts.

## Customisation and backups

Open the thread's **Prefs** tab and choose **Appearance and layout**, or press **Alt + Shift + D**.

Settings and recorded history are saved in your browser. They do not automatically transfer to another device or browser profile. Use **Preferences → More → Backup and restore** to export or restore a backup.
